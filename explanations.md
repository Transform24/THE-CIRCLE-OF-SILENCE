# Verse explanations for the Stillness Journey v8 (Grace's voice, drafted by Code)
Format: ## chapter | reference | on-screen title, then the two lines shown about 10 seconds after the verse.

## 1 | PSALM 46:10 | Be Still
Sit down. Put the phone away.
God is God whether or not you fix anything tonight.

## 2 | MATTHEW 11:28 | Come and Rest
You can come tired. You can come as you are.
He says it plainly: I will give you rest.

## 3 | ISAIAH 26:3 | Perfect Peace
A steady mind stays on Him.
That is where the peace comes from.

## 4 | PROVERBS 4:23 | Guard Your Heart
Everything you do flows from your heart.
Choose what you let in.

## 5 | MATTHEW 6:33 | Seek Him First
Seek Him first and the rest falls into place.
Start with this one quiet minute.

## 6 | PSALM 27:14 | Wait on the Lord
Waiting takes courage.
Take heart and wait again.

## 7 | ISAIAH 40:31 | Renewed Strength
Wait on the Lord and He renews you.
You will walk and not faint.

## 8 | ISAIAH 41:10 | Do Not Fear
God says He is with you.
He says He will hold you up. Believe Him.

## 9 | ZEPHANIAH 3:17 | He Rejoices Over You
The Lord is in the middle of your life.
He rejoices over you with singing.

## 10 | JOHN 14:27 | His Peace
Jesus leaves you His peace.
It does not depend on how the day goes.

## 11 | PSALM 19:14 | Acceptable Words
Pray the words you speak and the thoughts you keep.
Ask Him to make them pleasing to Him.

## 12 | PSALM 91:1 | The Secret Place
Stay close to Him and you are covered.
His shadow is a safe place to rest.

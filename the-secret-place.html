The Guilty Giver
She pours into everyone. She never stops to be filled.

You already know this one. You give and give and call it love, but underneath it there is guilt, not love. You feel guilty resting. You feel guilty saying no. You feel guilty wanting anything for yourself that does not also serve someone else.

I was her. This ministry was built on a day my body finally stopped letting me pour anymore, a broken ankle, of all things, was what it took for me to sit still long enough to hear that I had been giving from empty for years and calling it faith.

If this is you, you are not selfish for wanting more. You have just never been shown a version of giving that does not require you to disappear.

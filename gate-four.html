Tool Two

Giving Up Or Giving Over

These two things feel the same in the body.
They are not the same in the soul. Which are you living?

Burnout Disguised As Faith
Running on empty and calling it endurance.

Sometimes what looks like faithful perseverance is actually the body's unchosen surrender. It is simply out of anything left to give. There is no honor in collapsing quietly. God is not asking you to endure past the point of breaking. He is asking you to stop before you get there, and call that obedience, not failure.

"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."

Matthew 11:28, KJV

Proving Instead Of Abiding
Still trying to earn what was already given.

You cannot out-perform your way into a love that was never conditional. Proving keeps you at the edge of the room, always auditioning. Abiding means you have finally sat down. Seek first the kingdom, not the applause, not the achievement, and watch what is added without your striving.

"But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you."

Matthew 6:33, KJV

Controlling The Outcome
Gripping the plan because letting go feels like falling.

Control is often just fear wearing a productive disguise. You are not managing the outcome. You are exhausting yourself trying to. Surrender is not passivity. It is placing the plan in hands more capable than yours, and finally exhaling.

"And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose."

Romans 8:28, KJV

How to Pray

Before the practice, the pattern.

You have named what you are carrying. Before you build the habit of showing up in the silence, Jesus already gave you the shape of what happens once you are there. This is not a new technique. It is the pattern He taught His own disciples, and it is where intimacy with God is actually developed — not in how long you sit, but in how you pray while you do.

"But thou, when thou prayest, enter into thy closet, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret; and thy Father which seeth in secret shall reward thee openly."

Matthew 6:6, KJV

The Sermon on the Mount

The Pattern Jesus Gave Us

Matthew 6:9–13, KJV — prayed by Jesus' own disciples, and by every woman who has entered the secret place since.

"Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name."

Relationship first, then worship. You do not open with a request. You open by remembering whose daughter you are.

"Thy kingdom come, Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven."

Surrender. You lay down your agenda before you bring your requests.

"Give us this day our daily bread."

Dependence. Not a stockpile — today's portion, trusted for, one day at a time.

"And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors."

Mercy, given and received. You cannot carry both the offense and the peace.

"And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil."

Protection. You ask to be kept, not just to be forgiven after the fact.

Father. Surrender. Dependence. Mercy. Protection. This is the shape of every prayer you will pray inside your Secret Place practice.
